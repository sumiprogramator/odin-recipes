Steak

Description

The steak is beautifully seared, with a rich, caramelized crust that locks in its juices and gives it a deep, savory flavor. Sliced into thick pieces, it reveals a tender, slightly pink interior that looks both juicy and perfectly cooked. Coarse salt sprinkled on top adds a subtle crunch and enhances the natural taste of the beef.

It's served alongside golden roasted potatoes, crisp on the outside and soft on the inside, coated with herbs that add an earthy aroma. A side of blistered green peppers brings a touch of freshness and mild heat, balancing the richness of the meat. Together, the dish feels hearty, flavorful, and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • Beef steak
  • Baby potatoes
  • Green papers
  • OLive oil
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients | Wash the baby potatoes and cut them in halves if they're large. Pat the steak dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and black pepper.
  2. Cook the potatoes | Boil the potatoes in salted water for about 10–12 minutes until just tender. Drain them, then toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, and herbs (like rosemary or thyme).
  3. Roast or pan-fry the potatoes | Cook the potatoes in a hot pan or oven (around 200°C / 400°F) until golden and crispy on the outside, about 15-20 minutes.
  4. Cook the steak | Heat a pan until very hot. Add a bit of oil, then place the steak in the pan. Sear for about 2-4 minutes per side depending on thickness and desired doneness.
  5. Add butter and aromatics | In the last minute of cooking, add butter, crushed garlic, and herbs. Tilt the pan and spoon the melted butter over the steak repeatedly.
  6. Cook the peppers | In the same pan or a separate one, cook the green peppers with a bit of oil and salt until blistered and slightly charred.
  7. Rest the steak | Remove the steak from the pan and let it rest for 5 minutes to keep it juicy.
  8. Slice and serve | Slice the steak against the grain and serve with the crispy potatoes and peppers on the side.

Eggplant salad

Description

Romanian eggplant salad is made by roasting eggplants until soft and smoky, then peeling and draining them. The flesh is finely chopped or blended into a smooth texture.

It is mixed with sunflower oil, salt, and chopped onions, sometimes with a bit of lemon juice or mayonnaise. The salad is usually served as a creamy spread on bread.

Ingredients

  • Eggplants
  • Sunflower oil
  • Onion
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Roast the eggplants | Cook whole eggplants over an open flame or in the oven until the skins are charred and the inside is soft
  2. Peel and drain | Let them cool slightly, peel off the skins, and allow the flesh to drain to remove excess liquid
  3. Chop the eggplant | Finely chop with a knife or blend until smooth and creamy
  4. Mix with oil | Gradually add sunflower oil while stirring until the mixture becomes light and fluffy
  5. Add onion and seasoning | Stir in finely chopped onion and salt, plus optional lemon juice or mayonnaise to taste
  6. Serve | Chill slightly and serve as a spread on fresh bread
